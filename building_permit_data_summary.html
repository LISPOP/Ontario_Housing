Harmonization of names

The files have multiple names. This section harmonizes names for variables which are effectively the same. The first to be standardized are the dates.

library(janitor)
library(purrr)
city.list<-
  list("Toronto"=toronto, "Mississauga"=mississauga, "Burlington"=burlington, "Kitchener"=kitchener, "Hamilton"=hamilton, "Ottawa"=ottawa)
